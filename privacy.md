1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how H4rdik 2.0 handles information when users interact with the bot on Discord.

We respect user privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to operate the bot.

Depending on the features you use, H4rdik 2.0 may process:

- Discord User IDs

- Server configuration and settings
- Moderation records, such as warnings

Important: Only retain information that the bot actually collects or stores.

Information may be used to:

- Provide bot commands and features.
