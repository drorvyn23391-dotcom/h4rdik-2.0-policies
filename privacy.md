H4rdik 2.0 – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how H4rdik 2.0 handles information when users interact with the bot on Discord.

We respect user privacy and aim to collect and retain only the information necessary to operate our services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, H4rdik 2.0 may collect and store:

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server IDs
- Server configurations and settings
- User XP and leveling information
- Moderation records, including warnings
- Command-related information necessary for bot functionality

We do not intentionally collect Discord account passwords or login credentials.

3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

- Provide bot commands and features.
- Operate moderation and security systems.
- Maintain server configurations.
- Track XP, levels and user progress.
- Manage giveaways and other interactive features.
- Troubleshoot technical issues.
- Protect the bot against abuse and unauthorized activities.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate H4rdik 2.0.

5. Data Storage and Security

H4rdik 2.0 uses MongoDB to store necessary bot information, including user-related statistics, leveling data, moderation records and server configurations.

This information is stored outside Discord and is accessed only when required for bot functionality and administration.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the bot's functionality and maintain its services.

Users and server administrators may request the deletion of applicable stored information by contacting our support team.

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed where applicable.

Support Server: https://discord.gg/jPy6dhWA4M

7. Children's Privacy

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements applicable to their location.

8. Third-Party Services

H4rdik 2.0 relies on Discord for platform functionality and MongoDB for database storage.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, data access or deletion requests, contact:
